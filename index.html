UNMANNED AERIAL OPERATIONS // UKRAINE

Fly a reconnaissance-strike UAV. Find and identify armour, air defences and artillery through a gimballed thermal camera, and guide weapons onto them by laser.

CONTROLS
Touch (Android)

Drag: slew the camera
Pinch / + −: zoom
Tap: lock a track
TRK: lock at the crosshair
FIRE / WPN / FLR: fire, switch weapon, flares
Tap the map: move the camera there

Keyboard / Mouse

Drag or WASD/arrows: slew
Wheel or Q/E: zoom
Click / Space: lock track
F or Enter: fire   1/2: weapon
C: flares   T: thermal mode   O: orbit
H: help   Esc/P: pause

The laser guides your munitions to the crosshair until impact, so keep it on the target. Lock and zoom in on a target to get positive ID (PID). The mission fails if you go over the civilian-casualty limit.

Strap on the goggles and fly a 3D first-person FPV quad over the same village, fields and river. Throw flips, rolls, power loops and dives, thread gaps under the bridge and the power lines, and race the gates.

CONTROLS
Touch

Left stick: throttle (up/down) & yaw (left/right)
Right stick: pitch & roll
MODE: self-level / acro
Tap the view to recentre the sticks

Keyboard / Gamepad

W/S: throttle   A/D: yaw
Arrows or I/K/J/L: pitch & roll
Shift: boost   M: flight mode   R: recover
Gamepad: twin sticks (Mode 2), supported

Self-levelling holds the horizon for you; acro gives you full rate control for flips and loops but will not catch you. Tricks score more in a combo — keep them chained.

First-person-view strike sorties. Dive a one-way FPV drone onto enemy armour and air defences through analog goggles, dodging flak and wires, for cool, explosive effect. A work of fiction.

CONTROLS
Touch

Left stick: throttle & yaw
Right stick: pitch & roll
ARM / STRIKE: diving attack
BOOST: sprint

Keyboard / Gamepad

W/S/A/D + arrows: fly
Space: arm / commit the dive
Shift: boost   R: new drone
Gamepad twin sticks supported

Fly the drone into the target. Identify armour before you commit — hitting civilian traffic ends the sortie. Each lost drone is expended; you carry a limited number.

A work of fiction. Units, places and events are invented.
